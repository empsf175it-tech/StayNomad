Privacy & Guest Data Policy

Effective November 1, 2026 · Plain-language stewardship of your personal information.

Contents

1. Information We Collect

Just as we leave minimal footprints on mountain ridges and coastal dunes, StayNomad collects only the essential information required to host you safely and comfortably:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Your full name, email address, and mobile/WhatsApp number used to deliver offline trailhead maps and emergency weather alerts.
  • Reservation Preferences: Selected check-in/check-out dates, party count, pet notices, and optional dietary or occasion notes shared voluntarily with your host.
  • Statutory Check-In Records: Government ID verification where mandated by state hospitality and forest-buffer regulations at check-in.

2. How We Use Your Data

We never sell, rent, or broker guest profiles to advertising networks. Your data is used strictly to confirm availability, coordinate local steward transfers (such as shikara or 4x4 snow shuttles), issue tax invoices, and send optional New Moon Dispatches only when you explicitly subscribe.

3. Wilderness Host Sharing

When a reservation is confirmed, your host receives only your name, party count, arrival window, and phone number so they can pre-heat soaking tubs, prepare linen, and greet you at the trailhead. Payment card credentials are never shared with property hosts.

4. Browser Storage & Preferences

Instead of invasive third-party tracking cookies, this website uses lightweight local storage inside your own browser strictly to enhance your visit:

  • staynomad_theme — Remembers whether you prefer Golden-Hour Daylight or Night-Cabin Dark Mode.
  • staynomad_wishlist — Keeps your hearted cabins saved between visits.

5. Exact Cabin Coordinates & Guest Solitude

To protect both fragile habitats and the privacy of sleeping guests, public StayNomad pages display approximate valley or headland coordinates only. Exact turn-by-turn trailhead coordinates and brass lockbox codes are transmitted privately to verified guests 48 hours prior to arrival.

6. Your Privacy Rights & Data Contact

You may request export, correction, or complete deletion of your StayNomad account and reservation history at any time by writing to our Data Stewardship Desk at privacy@staynomad.com or via our Wilderness Concierge form.
