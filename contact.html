Frequently asked before departure

Everything you need to know about flexible cancellations, self check-in, bringing dogs, and off-grid comfort.

Cancel or move your dates free of charge up to 7 days prior to check-in for a full 100% refund. Cancellations between 7 days and 48 hours before arrival receive a 50% cash refund or a 100% flexible StayNomad credit valid across all 12 stays for twelve months.
Exactly 48 hours prior to arrival, we email and WhatsApp an offline-ready Trailhead Dossier containing pinned GPS coordinates, turn-by-turn milestone photos (so you never rely on cell bars), brass lockbox PINs, and your local steward’s direct phone number.
Seven of our twelve sanctuaries warmly welcome dogs (including Pine Hollow A-Frame, Moss & Stone Cottage, Ember Ridge, and Tideglass Beach Hut)—complete with paw towels and steel bowls. Simply check Pet-friendly on the Stays filter panel. Canopy treehouses and gazelle-corridor domes remain pet-free for wildlife safety.
We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and major domestic & international cards. Every booking calculator itemises your nightly rate, cabin linen & wood-prep fee, 12% stewardship fee, and 8% conservation & occupancy tax upfront—zero surprise checkout surcharges.
Absolutely. On StayNomad, “Off-grid” means silent solar-lithium microgrids rather than diesel generators—never cold showers. Every cold-climate stay features wood-burning stoves, radiant floor heating, or passive-solar insulation, and all Wi-Fi-tagged cabins deliver high-speed satellite or fiber internet.
Standard sedans, EVs, and hatchbacks comfortably reach 10 of our 12 properties. For Dune Whisper Camp (deep sand track) and heavy mid-winter snow weeks at Ember Ridge, your host includes a complimentary 4x4 transfer from a paved secure parking point.
Every reservation is protected by our Weather Assurance Guarantee: if official district authorities close highway or pass access to your stay, you can reschedule immediately at zero cost or claim a 100% refund—even on the morning of check-in.
Every pantry comes stocked with single-estate pour-over coffee, loose-leaf teas, local honey, sourdough or regional breads, olive oil, and seasoning essentials. At stays without full kitchens, seasonal breakfast baskets are included and regional home-cooked dinners can be pre-ordered 24 hours ahead.
