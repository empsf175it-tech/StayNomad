StayNomad began with one simple conviction: the rarest luxury left on earth is stepping onto a timber porch at dusk and seeing zero artificial lights between you and the horizon.

Our Origin & Mission

Why we reject mass-market glamping

Too many wild valleys have been scarred by multi-storey concrete resorts disguised as nature retreats. We built StayNomad as the antidote: every listing must stand lightly on reversible footings, celebrate vernacular craft—from Himalayan Kath-Kuni joinery to Konkan shell-lime plaster—and keep guest density radically low.

Guiding Compass

Four rules we never compromise

Reversible Footprints

Dark-Sky Reverence

Radical Privacy

The StayNomad Covenant

Our binding sustainability pledge

Published annually and written into every host partnership agreement we sign.

85%

Unbuilt Land Mandate

At least 85% of every host parcel remains unpaved native forest, dune grass, or wetland habitat in perpetuity.

100%

Solar & Closed-Loop Water

Zero noisy diesel generators. Every stay runs on solar storage, rainwater harvesting, and root-zone greywater gardens.

1%

Gross Revenue to Wild Corridors

One percent of every booking funds native sapling nurseries and community wildlife guardians—whether or not a stay is discounted.
